DOOMHud 1.0

Install DOOMHud.vl2 into the Tribes 2 base/ directory. The package auto-loads
the DOOM-style status bar and contains the DOOM graphics and GFT font sizes
8 through 32. The hook's ui.hud_get_value option (on by default in t2launch)
supplies the live health and energy values; no external executable, support.vl2,
loadout.cs, or xHealth/xEnergy globals are required.

The status bar is centered along the bottom edge and follows PlayGui resizing.
Weapon slots and ammo are updated from the normal Tribes 2 HUD messages. The
three keycard sockets are available to scripts through
$DOOMHud::BlueKey/$DOOMHud::YellowKey/$DOOMHud::RedKey; unset values keep them
hidden. The packaged DOOMHud.txt also serves as an example of selecting the
bundled font with <font:doom:18>.

Original DOOM graphics and font conversion credits are retained in README.md.
